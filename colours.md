# Colours for the PTF Brand
The brand palette sits within an analogous range in between blue and pink, creating a cohesive yet flexible spectrum of colours.

A mix of light, bright, and dark tones adds versatility across different applications, while maintaining the brand’s recognisability.

### Blue Tones
Light Blue: #ADEAF7  
Sky Blue: #33CCFF  
Rich Blue: #171BE5  
Navy Blue: #1A0566  

### Pink Tones
Light Fuchsia: #F2AAE2  
Hot Pink: #EB13B9  
Dark Magenta: #7D0E8C  

### Grey Tones
Coal: #141414  
White: #FFFFFF   
Off-White: #F5F5F5  
Light-Grey: #EAEAEB  
Grey: #DBDBDB  

## Combinations
To differentiate our funding tracks, we use a specific combination for each one as follows:
Innovation: Hot Pink/Navy Blue  
Up and Coming: Light Fuchsia/Dark Magenta  
Resilience: Sky blue/Rich Blue
